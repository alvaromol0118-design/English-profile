## Question 1 · What did you leave out?

In my first Spanish version I had my home address in the "Información" section. I removed it completely, so it does not appear in either version. A public profile can be read by anyone, including automatic programs, and a recruiter only needs my city and my email to contact me.

---

## Question 2 · What did you not translate?

I kept the word "responsive" in both versions. It is the standard technical term in web development, and developers in Colombia and in English-speaking countries use it every day. A literal translation like "adaptable al tamaño de pantalla" is longer and sounds unnatural in a technical profile.

---

## Question 3 · What was difficult?

> Spanish: Me encanta transformar ideas de diseño en código funcional y aprender continuamente sobre tecnologías web modernas.
>
> English: I like turning design ideas into working code and learning modern web technologies every day.

A word-by-word translation ("I love to transform ideas of design into functional code") sounds strange in English. I changed "transformar" to "turning", moved the adjective before the noun ("design ideas"), and used "working code" because it is more natural than "functional code". I also changed "continuamente" to "every day" to make the sentence shorter.
